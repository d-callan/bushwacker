### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

This manuscript introduces Bushwhacker, an information-theoretic phylogenetic graph induction method and lossless sequence compressor (`.bwz`) implemented in bit-parallel Rust and WebAssembly. Rather than selecting a parametric continuous-time Markov substitution matrix via AIC/BIC and searching over strictly bifurcating topologies, Bushwhacker prices discrete internal bipartitions, combinadic mutation site addresses, transition/transversion self-information weights, and lateral reticulate shunts in natural logarithmic units (nats) relative to a star-tree null baseline. The authors evaluate Bushwhacker across 22 synthetic AliSim regimes (220 alignments), 7,200 empirical and simulated nucleotide alignments from the Abadi et al. (2019) and Gerth (2019) model-selection archive, 4,159 eukaryotic single-gene alignments from six phylogenomic datasets (Zhou et al., 2018), 242 universal prokaryotic marker genes from GTDB Release 95, and empirical viral, mammalian (`SLC26A5` prestin), and mosaic recombinant alignments. Source code (`src/lib.rs`) and benchmark scripts are publicly available on GitHub alongside public OSF, Figshare, and GTDB data archives.

The core physical insight of the paper—that standard phylogenetic model selection ignores topological complexity and that topological conflicts between BIC-selected models and `GTR+I+G4` concentrate on near-zero-mutation branches (`ℓ_e L < 1.0`)—is persuasive and reinforced by a sharp code audit uncovering a deterministic PhyML `-o lr` BioNJ tie-breaking artifact in the `c1–c3` reference trees of Abadi et al. (2019). However, several mathematical inconsistencies, asymmetric baseline comparisons, and contradictions between the narrative and the manuscript's own tables undermine the broader performance claims. First, the two-part MDL equations contain mathematical errors: the integer code `L_N(m)` diverges under Kraft's inequality, the bipartition cost `L_topo(k_u, k_v)` in Equation (3) miscounts asymmetric splits and subtracts trivial split counts from a single binomial coefficient, and the linear marginal weight approximation in Equations (4)–(5) omits incremental integer-header costs, causing `G_MDL(e) = -0.65` nats and the exact two-part difference `T_star → T_split` (`64.75 → 64.70` nats, a `+0.05` nat compression gain) to have opposite signs in the worked Table 1 example (`m_e = 1`). Second, headline claims of superior topological accuracy rely on comparing multifurcating MDL trees against uncontracted binary ML trees (omitting bootstrap- or UFBoot-collapsed ML baselines) and selectively reporting unnormalized `RF_bin` while Table 3 Panel B shows Bushwhacker has the worst normalized Robinson-Foulds distance (`nRF_bin` and `nRF_≥1`) across the heterogeneous `c1` and `c2` corpora. Third, in the mammalian prestin case study (Figure 2B), four of the five codons attributed to the single lateral shunt between `N143` (Delphinidae) and `N192` (Rhinolophoidea) actually evolved on deeper ancestral branches—including `L497M`, which is shared by non-echolocating fruit bats (`Pteropus`), directly contradicting the text. These issues can be resolved through mathematical corrections to the code-length ledger, symmetric comparison against support-contracted ML trees, and tempering overstated benchmark narratives without generating new data.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **Topological discordance across substitution models (BIC vs. GTR+I+G4) concentrates on zero-mutation internal branches (`ℓ_e L < 1.0`), and Abadi et al.'s `c1–c3` benchmarks contain a deterministic BioNJ zero-branch artifact: [Well Supported].**  
  Across all 7,200 empirical alignments in the OSF archive, 52.2% of splits that conflict between BIC-selected models and `GTR+I+G4` (and 74.7% in the primary PlantDB, TreeBASE, and Selectome partitions) have expected mutation loads `ℓ_e L < 1.0`, and 96.4% are contracted by Bushwhacker. Furthermore, the forensic audit of `simulate_c1.py`–`simulate_c3.py` (Section 4.2, lines 740–753) convincingly demonstrates that running `phyml -o lr` fixed a deterministic Jukes-Cantor BioNJ topology with dozens of zero-length internal branches (`0.00000000`), inflating apparent PhyML agreement across substitution models via shared alphabetical starting-tree tie-breaking.

* **Combinadic MDL eliminates false-positive splits on null star radiations (`S08`) and very-low-stemminess trees where local likelihood-ratio tests (`--polytomy`) overfit: [Well Supported].**  
  On the 128-taxon null star tree (`S08`, Table 2), forced-binary ML methods produce 125.0 false splits (100% FPR) and `IQ-TREE 2 (--polytomy)` still outputs 74.8 false splits (59.8% FPR) because branch-local SH-aLRT/χ² tests do not penalize the multiple-testing search over `C(128, 2) = 8,128` candidate cherries. Bushwhacker (MDL) achieves 0.0 false positives across all replicates on `S08` and 98.8% precision (`0.18` FP/tree, `RF_≥1 = 3.69`) on very-low-stemminess trees (`>50%` of branches with `ℓ_e L < 1.0`, Table 3 Panel A).

* **The two-part MDL formulation in Equations (1)–(5) defines an exact normalized probability distribution via Kraft's inequality and contracts the `m_e = 1` quartet in Table 1 because resolving the split increases total description length: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Three internal mathematical errors invalidate this claim:
  1. In Section 2.1 (lines 126–127), the integer prefix code is defined as `L_N(m) = ln(1 + m) + 0.5 ln(1 + ln(1 + m))`. Because `∑_{m=0}^∞ (1 + m)⁻¹ (1 + ln(1 + m))⁻¹/² = ∞` (the integral `∫ dx / (x √(ln x)) = 2√(ln x)` diverges), `exp(-L_N(m))` violates Kraft's inequality (`∑_{m≥0} exp(-L_N(m)) = ∞`) and lacks Rissanen's normalization constant, contradicting the claim in Section 2.6 (lines 505–508) that `P(A | G) = exp(-L(A | G))` is an exact normalized probability distribution.
  2. In Section 2.3 and Table 1, the marginal leaf weight `w_c = ln((L_eff - m_c)/(m_c + 1))` (Equation 4) omits the incremental Rissanen integer cost `L_N(m_c + 1) - L_N(m_c) = 1.8212 - 1.4692 = 0.352` nats per leaf (`0.704` nats across `T1` and `T2`), and Equation (5) multiplies the first-step marginal savings `∆s = 5.28` linearly by `m_e` instead of summing diminishing combinadic increments `ln C(16, 2 + m_e) - ln C(16, 2)`. As a direct result, Table 1 is internally contradictory at `m_e = 1`: the `G_MDL(e)` column reports `-0.65` nats (`Contract`), whereas the adjacent `Exact Two-Part T_star → T_split` column reports `64.75 → 64.70` nats—showing that resolving the `m_e = 1` split actually *shortens* the exact two-part code by `0.05` nats (`64.70 < 64.75`). Similarly, at `m_e = 2` and `m_e = 3`, `G_MDL(e)` (`+1.00` and `+3.29` nats) disagrees with the exact two-part savings (`69.84 - 68.32 = +1.52` nats and `74.22 - 71.31 = +2.91` nats).
  3. Equation (3) defines `L_topo(k_u, k_v) = ln max(1.5, 0.5 C(k_u + k_v, k_u) - (k_u + k_v) + 1)`. For asymmetric splits (`k_u ≠ k_v`), swapping `u` and `v` changes the subset sizes, so there are `C(d, k_u)` bipartitions of sizes `{k_u, k_v}`, not `0.5 C(d, k_u)` (hence `L_topo(2, d - 2)` in Equation 3 yields `2 ln d - ln 4`, contradicting `2 ln d - ln 2` on line 189). Moreover, subtracting `(k_u + k_v) - 1` (the number of size-1 trivial splits across all `2^{d-1}` subsets) from a single binomial coefficient `C(d, k_u)` with `k_u ≥ 2` is combinatorially invalid: for `d = 5` (`k_u = 2, k_v = 3`), there are `C(5, 2) = 10` distinct cherry choices, yet Equation (3) evaluates `0.5(10) - 5 + 1 = 1` and floors the cost at `ln 1.5 ≈ 0.405` nats instead of `ln 10 ≈ 2.303` nats.

* **Bushwhacker achieves the lowest Robinson-Foulds error across the simulated Abadi et al. corpora (`sim_bic`, `c0`, `c1`, and `c2`) and improves species-tree concordance across the 4,159 eukaryotic genes of Zhou et al. (2018): [Overstated].**  
  The text selectively cites unnormalized `RF_bin` on subsets where heavy split contraction favors multifurcating trees while contradicting the normalized metrics in its own tables:
  1. Across all 5,901 `sim_bic` alignments (Table 3 Panel A), ` Bushwhacker (MDL)` has substantially higher (worse) error (`RF_bin = 10.51`, `nRF_bin = 0.2727`, `RF_≥1 = 6.87`, `nRF_≥1 = 0.2303`) than `IQ-TREE 2 (--polytomy)` (`RF_bin = 6.82`, `nRF_bin = 0.1404`, `RF_≥1 = 3.34`, `nRF_≥1 = 0.1147`), `FastTree 2` (`RF_bin = 7.24`, `RF_≥1 = 4.56`), and standard binary `IQ-TREE 2` (`RF_bin = 8.42`, `RF_≥1 = 5.75`).
  2. In Table 3 Panel B (`c0`, `c1`, `c2`), the text (lines 732–739) claims Bushwhacker ranks first on `RF_bin` and `RF_≥1`, but fails to note that on the normalized metrics `nRF_bin` and `nRF_≥1` in the very same table, `Bushwhacker (MDL)` ranks **last (worst)** among all evaluated methods in `c1` (`nRF_bin = 0.2578` vs. `0.1843` for `IQ-TREE 2 --polytomy`, `0.1995` for `FastTree 2`, and `0.2158` for binary `IQ-TREE 2`) and `c2` (`nRF_bin = 0.2616` vs. `0.1867`, `0.1999`, and `0.2180`), and also loses to `IQ-TREE 2 (--polytomy)` and `FastTree 2` on `nRF_bin` and `nRF_≥1` in `c0`.
  3. Across all 4,159 eukaryotic genes in Table 4 (`All Combined`, line 788), `Bushwhacker (MDL)` has *lower* mean concordance precision (`42.6%`) and *higher* (worse) normalized species-tree distance (`nRF_sp = 0.5447`) than both `Best-of-40 ML` (`51.2%` precision, `nRF_sp = 0.4885`) and `FastTree 2` (`47.8%` precision, `nRF_sp = 0.5221`). Subtracting `Discordant FP` from `Res` in Table 4 shows why: across all 4,159 genes, `Best-of-40 ML` recovers `82.2 - 43.79 = 38.41` species-concordant splits per gene, whereas `Bushwhacker (MDL)` recovers only `48.2 - 29.82 = 18.38` concordant splits per gene. Thus, of the `34.0` splits per gene contracted by Bushwhacker (`82.2 → 48.2`), `20.03` (`58.9%`) were actually concordant with the ASTRAL species tree and only `13.97` (`41.1%`) were discordant.
  4. Throughout Sections 4–6, the manuscript argues that MDL replaces bootstrap thresholds, yet never benchmarks `Bushwhacker (MDL)` against standard ML trees with low-support branches contracted by UFBoot (e.g., `<70%` or `<95%`) or SH-aLRT/aBayes, nor does it report `Bushwhacker_Binary` in Tables 3 and 4.

* **Stage 4 shunt peeling on mammalian `prestin` (`SLC26A5`) isolates a single lateral shunt between `N143` (Delphinidae) and `N192` (Rhinolophoidea) across five convergent echolocation codons while non-echolocating baleen whales and fruit bats retain ancestral states: [Fragile].**  
  While recovering monophyletic Chiroptera and Cetacea on `prestin.fasta` is a useful demonstration, the Stage 4 shunt attribution in Section 7.1 (lines 939–945) and Figure 2B/C contradicts the empirical alignment states displayed in Figure 2B:
  1. Figure 2B explicitly shows that at Codon 497 (`L497M`), both non-echolocating Old World fruit bats (`P. vampyrus` and `P. alecto`, Pteropodidae) carry the derived state `M (ATG)`, directly contradicting lines 944–945 and the Figure 2 legend ("whereas non-echolocating baleen whales (Mysticeti) and Old World fruit bats (Pteropodidae) retain the ancestral mammalian states (Figure 2B)"). Because all sampled bats share `M (ATG)`, `L497M` occurred on the ancestral stem of all Chiroptera, not on the `N192` (Rhinolophoidea) branch.
  2. Similarly, Figure 2B shows that `I384T`, `S392A`, and `L497M` are shared across all sampled Odontoceti (including sperm whale `P. catodon`, beluga `D. leucas`, and baiji `L. vexillifer`), meaning they occurred on the Odontoceti stem rather than the `N143` (Delphinidae) branch; and `N7T` and `S392A` are shared by vespertilionid bats (`M. lucifugus`, `E. fuscus`), not restricted to `N192` (Rhinolophoidea). Only 1 of the 5 codons (`G601A`) actually maps to the `(N143, N192)` branch pair. Aggregating non-coincident substitutions from distinct ancestral branches onto a single node pair `(N143, N192)` to accumulate `+44.58` nats of data savings against the `-36.78` nat header violates the branch-pair shunt formulation in Section 2.5.

* **Across 242 GTDB prokaryotic markers, MDL compressibility supports the Complexity Hypothesis and identifies 6,038 certified xenologous horizontal gene transfers: [Moderately Supported].**  
  The macro-evolutionary pattern in Table 5 Panel B—that ribosomal and translational markers exhibit higher phylogenetic compressibility (`ρ(A) = 31.2%`) and higher split precision (`48.20%`) than operational genes (`26.8%`) and aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases (`26.0%`, `43.33%` precision)—is consistent with the Complexity Hypothesis, and contracting unsupported deep splits sharply increases Four-Gamete taxonomic rank compatibility (`13.8% → 27.7%` for bacterial phyla; `67.6% → 92.3%` for genera). However, two caveats temper this section: (1) on total recovered species-tree splits across all 242 markers, `Bushwhacker (MDL)` recovers fewer than half as many true splits per gene as `FastTree 2` (`275.3` vs. `564.2`) at slightly lower overall precision (`46.9%` vs. `48.7%`), and `Bushwhacker_Binary` degrades sharply on deep prokaryotic alignments (`25.7%` precision vs. `42.2%` for `FastTree 2` on `bac120`); and (2) labeling all `6,038` single-gene nucleotide splits with `P_MDL ≥ 0.990` that nest 1–3 foreign species inside a clade as "MDL-certified xenologous transfer splits" ignores convergent GC/AT compositional bias (e.g., shared hyperthermophilic or halophilic nucleotide skew at third codon positions), unrecognized deep paralogy, and rate heterogeneity across billion-year divergences.

* **The `.bwz` phylogenetic codec outperforms general string compressors on clonal alignments and diagnoses recombination crossovers at `K = 2–3` breakpoints per genome: [Moderately Supported].**  
  On clonal viral, organelle, rRNA, and bacterial chromosome alignments, `.bwz` achieves `1.18×` to `1.90×` better compression than `gzip -9`, `bzip2 -9`, and `lzma -9` (Table 6). However, in the controlled recombination titration (Section 7.2, lines 1135–1137), the text states that at `K = 1` breakpoint per genome the Stage 4 network MDL (`12,191 B`) beats `lzma -9` (`14,428 B`) and that the crossover occurs at `K = 2–3`. Inspection of Table 6 (line 1191) shows that the actual `.bwz` executable file size at `K = 1` is `16,833 B`, which is already larger than `lzma -9` (`14,428 B`, `Ratio = 0.86× < 1.00×`). Thus, for the actual `.bwz` compressor, the crossover against `lzma -9` occurs at `K = 1` breakpoint, not `K = 2–3`. In addition, Table 6 shows that `.bwz` (which includes taxon headers, gap tracks, and the Newick tree) is smaller than the sequence-only `Net MDL` on several datasets (`InfluenzaA.fasta`: `2,247 B` vs. `2,377 B`; `prestin.fasta`: `7,536 B` vs. `7,890 B`), indicating that the `.bwz` arithmetic coder uses a richer context model (codon-phase and causal site-rate tracking) than the two-part `L_MDL` objective used for graph induction.

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Reconcile the Two-Part MDL Ledger, Prefix-Code Normalization, and Worked Example (Equations 1–5 and Table 1)
* **WHY:** Currently, `L_N(m)` diverges under Kraft's inequality, `L_topo(k_u, k_v)` in Equation (3) undercounts asymmetric splits and subtracts trivial splits from a single binomial term, and the linear marginal weight approximation `m_e ∆s` in Equation (5) contradicts the exact two-part code lengths in Table 1 (`m_e = 1` has `G_MDL = -0.65` nats while `T_star → T_split` drops from `64.75` to `64.70` nats).
* **WHAT:** Fix the prefix code for integers, correct the combinatorial bipartition count at degree-`d` hubs, and compute `G_MDL(e)` from the exact difference in branch description lengths `∑_c [L_branch(m_c, L_eff,c) - L_branch(m_c - m_{e,c}, L_eff,c)]` rather than a static first-derivative tangent approximation.
* **HOW:**
  1. Replace `L_N(m)` in Equation (1) with a Kraft-valid universal integer code, such as Rissanen's normalized log-star code `L*_N(m) = ln c + ln(1 + m) + ln(1 + ln(1 + m)) + ...` (`c ≈ 2.865`) or the standard Elias/Rissanen convergent form `ln(1 + m) + (1 + ε) ln(1 + ln(1 + m)) + ln c_ε` (`ε > 0`), so that `∑_{m≥0} exp(-L_N(m)) ≤ 1`.
  2. Replace Equation (3) with the exact count of unordered non-trivial splits at a degree-`d` node: either price the split across all `2^{d-1} - d - 1` non-trivial bipartitions (`L_topo(d) = ln(2^{d-1} - d - 1)`), or first encode the unordered size pair `{k_u, k_v}` (`1 ≤ k_u ≤ ⌊d/2⌋`, `k_u ≥ 2`) and then add `ln C(d, k_u)` when `k_u ≠ k_v` and `ln(0.5 C(d, k_u))` when `k_u = k_v`.
  3. In Equation (5), Table 1, and Figure 1C, include the exact combinadic and `L_N(m)` difference `L_branch(m_c + m_e, L_eff) - L_branch(m_c, L_eff)` on the child branches so that `G_MDL(e) ≡ L_MDL(A, T_star) - L_MDL(A, T_split)` holds as an exact algebraic identity across all rows of Table 1.

#### 2. Add Symmetric Support-Contracted ML Baselines and Report Both Binary and Multifurcating Modes Consistently
* **WHY:** Comparing a method that contracts 41%–52% of internal branches (`Bushwhacker MDL`) against uncontracted binary trees (`IQ-TREE 2`, `RAxML`, `FastTree 2`) conflates the benefit of collapsing low-signal branches with the accuracy of the tree search itself, especially since each wrong binary split incurs `2` RF units (`1 FP + 1 FN`) whereas a contracted split incurs `1` RF unit (`0 FP + 1 FN`).
* **WHAT:** Include standard post-hoc support contraction baselines (e.g., `IQ-TREE 2` with UFBoot `<70%` and `<95%` collapsed, and `FastTree 2` with Shimodaira-Hasegawa local support `<0.70` and `<0.90` collapsed) alongside `Bushwhacker_Binary` in Tables 2, 3, 4, and 5.
* **HOW:** Using the existing tree outputs from the Abadi/Gerth and Zhou et al. runs, collapse internal branches below standard support thresholds (`0.70`, `0.90`, `0.95`) and compute `Res / True`, `FP`, `TP` (recovered true splits), `Precision`, `Recall`, `RF`, and `nRF`. In addition, explain why `RF_bin` and `nRF_bin` rank methods in opposite directions in Table 3 Panel B (`c0`, `c1`, `c2`), and temper the Abstract and Section 4.2 claims accordingly.

#### 3. Correct the Ancestral State Attribution in Stage 4 Shunt Peeling on Mammalian Prestin
* **WHY:** In Figure 2B, four of the five sites attributed to the `N143 (Delphinidae) ↔ N192 (Rhinolophoidea)` lateral shunt (`N7T`, `I384T`, `S392A`, `L497M`) actually occur on deeper ancestral clades (all Odontoceti or all Chiroptera, including non-echolocating fruit bats at `L497M`). Allowing Stage 4 to pool mutations that occurred on ancestor edges `e_1 ≠ e_2` onto a single descendant node pair inflates the shunt gain (`+44.58` nats).
* **WHAT:** Restrict Stage 4 shunt evaluation between branch `u` and branch `v` strictly to mutations inferred by Sankoff-Fitch (or marginal ancestral reconstruction) to have occurred *on* branches `u` and `v` themselves, and correct the text description of Figure 2B.
* **HOW:** In Stage 4 (`src/lib.rs`), verify that candidate site `s ∈ S_{u,v}` undergoes a state transition between parent and child on both edge `u = (p_u, c_u)` and edge `v = (p_v, c_v)` rather than merely sharing a derived state at nodes `c_u` and `c_v` inherited from deeper ancestors. Re-compute the prestin Stage 4 ledger on the exact edge transitions and revise lines 939–945 and the Figure 2 caption to reflect that `Pteropus` shares `L497M` and that `I384T`/`S392A`/`L497M` arise on the Odontoceti stem.

#### 4. Control for Compositional Bias in GTDB HGT Detection and Benchmark Domain-Specific MSA Compressors
* **WHY:** Third-codon-position GC/AT skew across thermophilic and halophilic prokaryotes can generate high-MDL-gain nucleotide splits that mimic horizontal gene transfer, and comparing `.bwz` only against generic byte-stream compressors (`gzip`, `bzip2`, `lzma`) omits specialized genomic/MSA codecs.
* **WHAT:** Filter or stratify the `6,038` putative GTDB xenologous transfers by first+second codon positions (or amino-acid translation) and GC-content divergence, and align the Section 7.2 recombination crossover narrative with Table 6.
* **HOW:**
  1. Re-evaluate the `6,038` candidate HGT splits in `bac120` and `ar122` after stripping third codon positions (or computing Galtier-Gouy/RY-coded MDL gains) to report what fraction of putative inter-phylum jumps survive after removing synonymous GC-bias convergence.
  2. In Section 7.2 (lines 1134–1139), state explicitly that the executable `.bwz` archive (`16,833 B`) crosses above `lzma -9` (`14,428 B`, `Ratio = 0.86×`) at `K = 1` breakpoint per genome, while the theoretical network MDL (`12,191 B`) crosses above `lzma -9` at `K = 2`, and clarify why `.bwz` is smaller than `Net MDL` on clonal alignments due to codon-phase arithmetic coding.
